# Working on agent-pr-gate

Read CONTRIBUTING.md before changing files or drafting a PR.
Follow its four-question gate: ask the human for missing answers and wait.
Do not supply or infer the human's answers yourself.
Reuse their explicit answers for this contribution. Ask for updates if the goal changes.

Keep claims tied to public sources. Distinguish a documented problem from evidence that the gate works.
Do not imply that a cited maintainer endorses this project.
Use public or synthetic examples. Keep secrets and private contributor answers out of published material.
